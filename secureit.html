SecureIt

Encrypted collections for keys, logins, private notes, and ideas.

Create your SecureIt vault

Your passphrase encrypts SecureIt in this browser before anything is saved. Centralis cannot read or recover your items without your passphrase or recovery key.

Unlock SecureIt

Enter your SecureIt passphrase. You only need this when opening SecureIt after a page load.

Reset passphrase with recovery key

This preserves your encrypted items and replaces the passphrase used to unlock this vault.

Save this recovery key

This is the only way to reset your SecureIt passphrase if you forget it. Store it somewhere outside Centralis.
